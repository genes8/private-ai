# Strategic Programming Resources

## Knowledge

- [Google Engineering Practices: Small CLs](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/developer/small-cls.html)
  Use for: learning how senior engineers split work into reviewable, reversible, low-risk changes.
- [Michael Nygard: Documenting Architecture Decisions](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions)
  Use for: lightweight decision records that preserve context, tradeoffs, status, and consequences.
- [Architectural Decision Records](https://adr.github.io/)
  Use for: common vocabulary, templates, and further reading on decision logs and architectural knowledge management.
- [Google SRE Book: Postmortem Culture](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/)
  Use for: turning failures into durable system learning, with emphasis on blameless analysis and action items.
- [The Practical Test Pyramid](https://martinfowler.com/articles/practical-test-pyramid.html)
  Use for: designing QA strategy across unit, integration, contract, UI, end-to-end, acceptance, and exploratory tests.
- [StaffEng: Staff Archetypes](https://staffeng.com/guides/staff-archetypes/)
  Use for: understanding strategic technical work patterns such as Tech Lead, Architect, Solver, and Right Hand.
- [Will Larson: Writing an Engineering Strategy](https://lethain.com/eng-strategies/)
  Use for: learning how to write coherent engineering strategy from diagnosis, policies, and actions.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Software Engineering Stack Exchange](https://softwareengineering.stackexchange.com/)
  Use for: testing architectural reasoning, tradeoff framing, and design alternatives in a moderated Q&A format.
- [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/)
  Use for: industry-grounded engineering leadership examples, incident writeups, and software delivery practices.
- [LeadDev](https://leaddev.com/)
  Use for: talks and articles on technical leadership, staff engineering, team execution, and engineering strategy.
